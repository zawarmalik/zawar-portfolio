## 💡 Quick Tips by Generator Tool
- **Midjourney**: Keep the `--ar 16:9 --v 6.0` flag at the end. For hero photo, use image-to-image by uploading your portrait URL first.
- **ChatGPT / DALL-E 3**: You can omit `--ar 16:9` and simply add "in 16:9 widescreen format, photorealistic, no text".
- **Google Gemini / Imagen 3**: Paste prompt directly as written; Imagen 3 preserves lighting and photorealism exceptionally well.
- **Ideogram**: Choose Style: 'General' or 'Realistic', and select 16:9 aspect ratio in the UI.
